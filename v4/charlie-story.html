Our story

For Charlie

When I met Charlie, I didn’t realize how much he would shape my life. He wasn’t just a pet — he was my constant, my teacher in patience and responsibility, and the one who quietly raised the bar for how deeply you can show up for another being.

Sixteen and a half years

Charlie was a rescue with a complicated medical history from the start. Over 16.5 years, we navigated chronic conditions, sudden flare-ups, and long stretches of uncertainty.

What made the difference wasn’t just extraordinary veterinarians — it was learning how to show up as a thoughtful advocate in his care. I kept records, paid close attention to every detail, and worked alongside his vet team so he could keep living a full, joyful life.

“BarkLens is the thing I wished for in every waiting room of those sixteen and a half years.”

Why BarkLens exists

Grief is tough. I built BarkLens this past year as a project dedicated to Charlie. I wanted to build the thing I wish I had while he was alive. BarkLens is the thing I wished for in every waiting room of those sixteen and a half years.

Until recently, this couldn’t be built: something to hold a dog’s entire medical history, carrying it into every answer, paired with real veterinary sources — not some random blog. It exists in Charlie’s honor. It exists because it helps others and it keeps me close to his memory.

I started this to honor Charlie. I’m finishing it for all of you reading this now. This is a tool I wish I had for Charlie and it’s now something I am proud to have finished for you and your dog.

Ben Singer
Charlie’s Dad
Founder, BarkLens
