Privacy

Your inbox is not our business model.

The plain-English version first. The formal version below it.

We read vet mail, nothing else.

BarkLens searches your Gmail for mail from veterinary senders. Your other email is never opened, stored, or scanned.

You approve what stays.

Every record BarkLens finds is shown to you first. Approve it and it joins his chart; skip it and it’s forgotten.

Never sold. Never shared.

No ads, no data brokers, no “partners.” Membership is the product — your data isn’t.

Leave with everything.

Export his chart anytime. Close your account and the records are deleted — not archived, deleted.

The formal version

Data we collect. Account details you provide (name, email), records you approve into your dog’s chart, and questions you ask. Mock copy — final policy to be drafted with counsel before launch.

Gmail access. Read-only scope, restricted to veterinary senders you confirm. Access tokens are revocable from your Google account at any time. Mock copy.

Storage & security. Records encrypted at rest and in transit. Access limited to systems that build your dog’s chart. Mock copy.

Deletion. Account deletion removes charts, records, and question history within 30 days. Mock copy.
