Frequently asked questions

Before you join.

What happens when the trial ends?

We email you before it does, and again before we charge you. If you cancel, you keep your records and your export.

What if I cancel later?

You keep your chart and your export. Features stop. History doesn’t.

Do I need a card to start the trial?

If you start through the App Store or Google Play, the payment method already on your store account is used. Either way, you are not charged during the seven days, we email you before the trial ends, and you can cancel any time before then at no cost.

Can I add someone who helps care for my dog?

Yes, and it’s free. Send them a link and they can see today’s medications, log a dose, and reach your emergency contacts. You can revoke it whenever you want.

Is there a plan for more than four dogs?

Not yet. If you’re fostering or running a rescue, write to hello@barklens.com — we’d rather hear from you than guess.

BarkLens uses AI and can make mistakes. It does not diagnose, and it is not a substitute for veterinary care. Anything it tells you about your dog’s health traces to a source you can check.
