## The short version

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does BarkLens use AI? | Yes. AI reads your records and photos, answers your questions, and keeps your dog's summary up to date. **Answers are generated by AI.** |
| Do you ask first? | Yes. Nothing is sent to an AI service until you tap **Allow** on the AI data sharing screen. |
| Which AI? | Google Gemini, xAI Grok and OpenAI, reached through Vercel AI Gateway. |
| Can I turn it off? | Yes, any time, in **Settings > Data & usage > AI data sharing**. |
| Is my data used to train AI? | We use paid API access, not consumer chat accounts. We plan to turn on a gateway setting that blocks any provider that trains on submitted content. Until it is on, we rely on each provider's API terms. |
| Does a vet check my answers? | **No.** A licensed veterinarian has reviewed the safety guidelines for our app, not your answers. |
| Is this veterinary advice? | **No.** It is education and record-keeping. Your vet decides. |
| Where is my data stored? | In Sydney, Australia, with our database provider, Supabase. Some vendors, including the AI services, process it in the United States and other countries. See Privacy Policy §2.12 and §7. |
| Can I delete it? | Yes, any item or your whole account, at any time. |

## 1. Your permission

- **Before any data goes to an AI service,** BarkLens shows the AI data sharing screen. It lists what we send, why, and who receives it.
- **Allow** turns AI features on. **Not now** keeps them off. Both buttons are the same size.
- **If you tap Not now,** you can still upload and view your dog's records, see History, and use Care Circle and reminders. AI features stay off: Chat, BarkLens capture reading, Vet questions, Closer look, Trends, and pulling lab values from uploads.
- **To turn it off later,** go to Settings > Data & usage and switch off AI data sharing. AI features turn off, and BarkLens stops sending new data to AI services. Your records stay saved.
- **To turn it back on,** switch it on in Settings > Data & usage, or tap "Turn on AI" on any AI feature. You will see the AI data sharing screen again.
- **We ask again** if we add an AI service or use your data for a new purpose.

## 2. What we send

These are the only kinds of your data we send to AI services:

- Vet records and lab reports you upload or import
- Photos you take or upload
- Your chat messages
- Your dog's details: name, breed, age, weight, conditions, meds

With a question, we also send passages from our own library. That is our content, not yours.

We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for ads.

## 3. Why, and when

| When | What is sent | Why | Who receives it |
|---|---|---|---|
| You upload or import a record or photo | The file or image, and its text | To read it and pull out lab values | Google Gemini |
| You take a photo | The photo | To check it is clear enough to read | Google Gemini |
| You ask a question | Your question, related parts of your dog's records and details, and passages from our library | To answer your questions | xAI Grok writes the answer. Google Gemini reads check-in photos and names the chat. OpenAI helps find related records and passages and suggests follow-up questions. |
| After a chat | The conversation | To note facts for later answers (for example, your dog's medicines) | OpenAI |
| About every 12 hours | Records and details already on file for each active dog | To keep your dog's summary up to date | Google Gemini |

## 4. Who receives it

| Service | What it does in BarkLens |
|---|---|
| **Google Gemini** | Reads records and photos |
| **xAI Grok** | Writes chat answers |
| **OpenAI** | Search and chat memory |

All requests go through **Vercel AI Gateway** (Vercel Inc.), which passes them to the service.

## 5. Photos and records

- We read **veterinary records, lab reports, food labels, medication and supplement labels, product packaging, meal photos, and stool photos.**
- We do **not** assess photos of your dog's body: skin, coat, eyes, ears, teeth, wounds, lumps, or posture.
- Reading is automatic and can be wrong. **Check any number that matters against the original.** Each value links back to its source.
- Uploading and viewing records is free. Photo capture for stool, food, and medicines is part of BarkLens+.

## 6. Gmail import (optional)

- Read-only. We can't send, delete, or change email.
- We search only for the record types and words you choose, and never spam or trash.
- We import only messages you select, and only their PDF attachments.
- We don't store email bodies.
- Imported PDFs are records. If AI data sharing is on, they are read like any other upload (section 3).
- Our use of Gmail data follows Google's API Services User Data Policy, including its **Limited Use** rules. **We do not use Gmail data to train AI models.**
- You can disconnect at any time. Records you imported stay until you delete them.

## 7. What a veterinarian reviewed, and what they didn't

|---|---|
| The safety guidelines for our app (when to contact a vet) | Your answers |
| — | The sources in our library |

## 8. Not veterinary advice

- BarkLens does not diagnose, prescribe, set doses, or say your dog is "fine."
- BarkLens does not watch your dog. **No alert does not mean no problem.**
- **In an emergency, call your vet or an emergency animal hospital now.** Don't wait for the app.
- AI can make mistakes, even when it cites a real source. Read the source, and ask your vet.

## 9. How long we keep things

| Data | How long |
|---|---|
| Records, photos, chats, and notes drawn from chats | Until you delete them, or close your account |
| After you close your account | 30-day grace period; export for 90 days; then deleted (Privacy §6) |
| Gmail connection and token | Until you disconnect |
| Gmail import history (searches used, record types, match count, imported message IDs) | Deleted when you disconnect Gmail |
| Copies held by AI services | Zero data retention is turned on for all AI calls through Vercel AI Gateway, so AI services do not keep copies of your requests. |
| Backups and system logs | 30–90 days, rolling |

## 10. Your choices

- Turn AI data sharing on or off in Settings > Data & usage.
- Delete any record, photo, or chat in the app.
- Disconnect Gmail at any time.
- Export your lab results as PDF.
- Ask us anything at **info@barklens.com**.
