# BarkLens Legal Documents Requested for Launch

Please provide final, counsel-approved documents or language for the items below.

## Required website documents

1. **Terms of Use**
   - Nature of the BarkLens service
   - No veterinarian-client-patient relationship
   - No diagnosis, prognosis, treatment plan, dosing, or clinical recommendation
   - Emergency-use warning
   - No detection, alerting, notification, or timeliness guarantee
   - User-provided data and photographed-document limitations
   - Veterinary-source limitations and accuracy language
   - Care Team access, user responsibility, and revocation
   - Acceptable use
   - Warranty disclaimer, liability limits, indemnification, governing law, and disputes

2. **Privacy Policy**
   - Account, waitlist, record, photo, document, question, and technical data
   - Purposes and lawful processing basis where applicable
   - AI, storage, analytics, hosting, and email service providers
   - Care Team sharing
   - Data retention, export, correction, deletion, and account closure
   - State and international privacy rights for the jurisdictions BarkLens will serve
   - Security, incident notification, children, and contact language

3. **Subscription and billing terms**
   - Plus and Family plan definitions
   - Trial length and conversion
   - App Store and Google Play billing
   - Renewal, cancellation, refunds, and price changes
   - What remains available after cancellation, including record export

4. **Email and waitlist terms**
   - Marketing-email consent language
   - Required sender identification and postal-address language
   - Unsubscribe language and suppression-record retention
   - Founding-member offer and referral-program terms

5. **Cookie and analytics position**
   - Whether a separate Cookie Policy is required
   - Consent-banner requirements by launch jurisdiction
   - Approved Google Analytics or Vercel Analytics disclosure

## Product facts counsel should use

- BarkLens accepts owner-directed uploads and photographs. No Gmail integration is currently offered.
- BarkLens organizes records and answers questions using a dog’s history and published veterinary sources.
- Every source is reviewed by a licensed veterinarian. Generated answers are not individually reviewed by a veterinarian.
- BarkLens does not diagnose.
- BarkLens does not guarantee detection, alerts, notifications, completeness, or timeliness.
- Users own their records and can export them, including after cancellation.
- Care Team members can receive access chosen by the account owner.
- Mailgun manages the waitlist and welcome email.

These materials must be reviewed and approved by qualified counsel before the website is made public or search indexing is enabled.
