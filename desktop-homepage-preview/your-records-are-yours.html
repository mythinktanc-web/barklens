Your records

Your records are yours.

Nothing you add is ever taken away. Export everything, anytime. A chart never expires.

That’s the whole promise, and it gets its own page because in this category it needs saying out loud.

Why this needs to be a promise

Owners in this market have been burned. Apps have shut down and taken years of records with them, with no way to export. Clinic portals hold your dog’s history but won’t let you edit it or take it with you, because the records live in the clinic’s software rather than yours. People have gone back to spreadsheets, and honestly, we understand why.

So we’re being specific rather than reassuring.

What that means concretely

You can export everything, at any time, in a format you can open and hand to a vet.

Cancelling doesn’t delete your dog. If you stop paying, you keep your records and your export. Features go away. History doesn’t.

We don’t expire charts. Not after a year, not after a subscription lapses, not after a dog dies. Especially not then.

Nothing you add is removed to sell you an upgrade. No record is held behind a paywall you already filled yourself.

What you add

BarkLens reads only the records you choose to add.

It keeps the lab report, visit summary, and values inside them with the original record.

Nothing is added silently. You review each record before it becomes part of your dog’s chart.

BarkLens keeps only the records you approve.

Your data is not the product

Your dog’s health records are not sold, and they are not sold in aggregate either. That distinction matters, because “we don’t sell your data” is often true in a narrow sense and false in every sense that counts.

The internet knows dogs. BarkLens knows yours.

BarkLens organizes your dog's health records and answers questions from that history and published veterinary sources. It does not diagnose. Clinical decisions are made by your veterinarian.
