Their bloodwork doesn’t.

75%

of kidney function typically lost before clinical signs appear

Kidneys have enormous spare capacity. That’s a gift — and it’s why the Merck Veterinary Manual notes that clinical signs of chronic kidney disease generally don’t appear until at least 75% of kidney function is gone. Your dog isn’t being stoic. The body is genuinely covering for itself, right up until it can’t.

That’s the whole reason a lab report matters more than how they look on a Tuesday. The numbers move on their own schedule, in their own units, on the range their own lab printed — and they were doing it in a PDF in your inbox whether anyone read it or not.

One draw is a dot.
Three draws is a direction.

A single value tells you where they were that morning. The same value across three years tells you a story: steady, drifting, or climbing. BarkLens puts every draw on one line, each judged against the range printed on its own report — so the direction is the part you can actually see.

Where the answers come from.

The library

Every general claim BarkLens makes traces to a verified veterinary source — clinical practice guidelines, veterinary manuals, peer-reviewed journals. Not scraped, not summarized from the open web. Read, verified, and stored word-for-word with the page it came from.

The verification

Every stored passage is checked character-for-character against the original document. When a source contradicts itself or prints something ambiguously, the claim is held rather than published. Nothing reaches an answer on a maybe.

The review

A licensed veterinarian reviews the source library and the safety rules that govern every answer. Not your dog’s results — those belong to your own vet. The standards behind the answers.

Your dog’s own records

Every value read against the range printed on its own report. Every vet note quoted exactly as written. The record leads; the science explains.
