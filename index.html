Where the answers come from.

The library

Every general claim BarkLens makes traces to a verified veterinary source — clinical practice guidelines, veterinary manuals, peer-reviewed journals. Not scraped, not summarized from the open web. Read, verified, and stored word-for-word with the page it came from.

The verification

Every stored passage is checked character-for-character against the original document. When a source contradicts itself or prints something ambiguously, the claim is held rather than published. Nothing reaches an answer on a maybe.

The review

A licensed veterinarian reviews the source library and the safety rules that govern every answer. Not your dog’s results — those belong to your own vet. The standards behind the answers.

Your dog’s own records

Every value read against the range printed on its own report. Every vet note quoted exactly as written. The record leads; the science explains.
